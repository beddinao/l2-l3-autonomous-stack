## *`Topology 03: Virtual LANs and 802.1Q Trunking`*

#### OVERVIEW
```text
This topology transitions from a flat network into a logically segmented architecture. 
By implementing Virtual Local Area Networks (VLANs), a single physical switching infrastructure 
is partitioned into multiple, completely isolated broadcast domains. It introduces the IEEE 802.1Q 
standard to multiplex these distinct networks across a single physical wire (a Trunk).
```

#### PART 01, INFRASTRUCTURE and VLAN PROVISIONING

```text
- Deploys four end-hosts across two separate geographic nodes, connected by two Cisco switches.
- Provisions two distinct logical networks in the switch databases: 
  VLAN 10 (BLUE_NET) and VLAN 20 (RED_NET).
- Host-1 and Host-2 belong to VLAN 10 (10.0.10.0/24).
- Host-3 and Host-4 belong to VLAN 20 (10.0.20.0/24).
```

#### PART 02, ACCESS PORTS and IP ADDRESSING

```text
- Configures static IPv4 addressing on all four Alpine containers.
- Maps specific switch interfaces to their respective VLANs using "switchport mode access".
- Switch-1 Gi0/1 and Switch-2 Gi0/1 are locked to VLAN 10.
- Switch-1 Gi0/2 and Switch-2 Gi0/2 are locked to VLAN 20.
- Access ports inherently strip VLAN tags before delivering frames to the end-hosts, 
  keeping the endpoints completely unaware of the virtualization layer.
```

#### PART 03, TRUNK LINK CONFIGURATION (802.1Q)

```text
- Configures the central link (Gi0/0 to Gi0/0) as a 802.1Q Trunk.
- Unlike access ports, a trunk port carries traffic for multiple VLANs simultaneously.
- Forces the usage of the IEEE dot1q encapsulation standard.
- Applies a security perimeter by strictly limiting the allowed VLANs on the trunk to 10 and 20, 
  preventing unauthorized broadcast domains from traversing the core link.
```

#### PART 04, VLAN 10 DATA PLANE VALIDATION

```text
- Validates intra-VLAN reachability: host-1 (10.0.10.1) successfully pings host-2 (10.0.10.2).
- Validates inter-VLAN isolation: host-1 attempting to ping host-3 (10.0.20.1) returns a 
  "Network unreachable" error. Without a Layer 3 routing device, the switch physically prevents 
  VLAN 10 from talking to VLAN 20.
```

```
- The packet capture on the core trunk explicitly shows the 802.1Q tag insertion.

```

#### PART 05, VLAN 20 DATA PLANE VALIDATION

```text
- Mirrors the validation for the second broadcast domain.
- host-3 (10.0.20.1) successfully pings host-4 (10.0.20.2), confirming end-to-end reachability.
- Ping attempts to VLAN 10 are similarly blocked by the strict Layer 2 boundaries.
```
